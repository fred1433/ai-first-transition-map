# Please list the logs that we could use the daily logs for?

Publicly available documentation, kept here as the evidence behind the analysis in this repository.

As we mentioned in another of our articles, the daily logs are concise, documented records that capture essential details of the day’s work on a construction site. They typically include information about crew activities, weather conditions, equipment usage, materials delivered or used, any incidents or safety issues, and a summary of the progress made. These logs are often filled out by site superintendents or project managers. It is not uncommon for Lead Guys, Foremen and Supervisors to fill the Daily logs.

Logs that we could use the Daily Logs for

Under Details

Activities done, progress of work log

Under Details populate the “Tasks Performed”

Delays log

Weather Delays

Schedule Delays

Jobsite Conditions Log

Weather Log

Under People

People’s notes log

Subcontractors on site and hours worked log

Employees on site log

Under Materials

Materials used log

Materials delivered log

Materials Notes Log

Under Equipment

Equipment log

Equipment Delivered log

Equipment notes log

In order to get all the listed above logs, go to the Reports Modules and click on the “Create a New Custom Report” then select the option “Daily Logs Report”.

This option will allow you to customize all the information within the Daily log and how you want to see it. This can be done by adding and removing columns.

It is very important to keep in mind that as best practice, all the fields in the daily logs should be populated in order to get more detailed reports.
