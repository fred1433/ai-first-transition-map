# How do I update the Daily Log status ?
Source: https://kb.contractorforeman.com/knowledge-base/how-do-i-update-the-daily-log-status/
Read at: 2026-09-18T12:53:19.614Z
Extractor: 1.0.0 (#eckb-article-content-body)
Publicly available documentation, kept here as the evidence behind the analysis in this repository.

To update the Daily Log status, go to the Daily Log Module and open the daily log in question.

Once there, right below the title of the Project you will see the status of the Daily Log. Click on it to see a drop down menu and pick the status of your preference.

In Progress: the Daily Log is still open and in use

Deferred: Started working on the Daily Log and had to postpone it

Complete: No more notes need to be added to the Daily Log
