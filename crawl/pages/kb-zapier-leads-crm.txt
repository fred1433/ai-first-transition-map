# Can I send Leads to my CRM or email system using Zapier?
Source: https://kb.contractorforeman.com/knowledge-base/can-i-send-leads-to-my-crm-or-email-system-using-zapier/
Read at: 2026-09-18T12:56:03.319Z
Extractor: 1.0.0 (#eckb-article-content-body)
Publicly available documentation, kept here as the evidence behind the analysis in this repository.

Yes, the leads sent to the CRM can be sent to Contractor Foreman using Zapier integrations. To set it up, you can go to the Menu>Settings and Support>Settings>System Settings>Integrations and click on Zapier to get started.

After you click on the link, it will take you to Zapier to log in and start exploring your options.
