The integration with Zapier makes it easy for you to send data from Contractor Foreman to other systems (such as MailChimp, Constant Contact, Zoho, Dropbox, OneDrive, and more). Although this process is fairly easy, we are unable to provide support for any settings or configuration outside of our system. Zapier will be able to assist with those questions.

Getting Started: If you do not already have an account, you will need to create a free Zapier account at https://zapier.com/sign-up

From your Contractor Foreman account, go to Menu > Settings > Integration > Zapier

From there, click on the Integration URL

Shortly after you’ll be asked to copy the API key displayed on the screen.

Click on the Zapier App link given there

Accept Invitation

Go to Zapier.com and login (or create a free account if you do not have one)

Click on New Zaps (on the left) and then click Create Zap (on the right)

In the Trigger box, search for and select Contractor Foreman.

Select “Lead” from the Trigger Event list.

Click Continue

Click “Sign in to Contractor Foreman”

Paste the API Key copied earlier into the popup.

Click “Test Trigger”

If you see, sample Lead details – You’re almost done.

Now continue and select Action.

Search “MailChimp”

Selection Action (Create New Lead or Update)

Sign into MailChimp

Map the fields
